Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Paul and Pen ("we") publishes coloring books. This policy explains what happens to information on this website and in the tools we use to share our own content on social media.

This website

This site does not use cookies, analytics, accounts or forms, and does not collect personal information. Free sample pages are downloaded directly with no sign-up. Links to Amazon, Instagram, YouTube and Pinterest take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Our social media publishing tool

We use a private tool, authorised only by us, to publish and schedule our own original content (Pins, videos and posts) to our own Pinterest, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It does not access, collect, store or share data about other users. Access tokens are stored only on our own computer and can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's settings.
